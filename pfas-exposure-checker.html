What This Checks

Patterns the tool looks for.

This checker asks four short questions: where the exposure came from, when, where you lived or worked, and whether there is a related diagnosis. It then matches your answers to the case categories most often tied to that pattern.

  • Exposure source — drinking water, AFFF firefighting foam, occupational, or layered
  • Approximate timeframe of exposure or residence
  • State, because deadlines and claim rules vary
  • Diagnosed conditions commonly cited in current PFAS litigation

After Your Result

What happens next.

If the checker points to a possible match, you can request a free case review. Your information is used to match your request to an active lawsuit category and to a participating law firm in your state.

If there appears to be a possible fit, David refers your request to a participating law firm, and that firm may contact you to ask for more information.

No attorney-client relationship is formed unless and until you sign an agreement directly with a law firm.

ny S 9051|New York AI Companion Law for Minors|Companies may not offer anyone under 18 an AI companion with any of 12 unsafe features. The state attorney general enforces it, with fines up to $25,000 per violation.|2027-01-01
ga SB 540|Georgia Chatbot Safety Law|Chatbot companies must tell users they are talking to AI. They must block sexual content and pretend romance with minors, and keep a plan for users who mention self-harm.|2027-07-01
nj A 5607|New Jersey Chatbot Liability Bill|This bill would treat a chatbot as a product. The company would be responsible for injuries its chatbot causes, and an injured person could sue.
ca SB 1119|California Adam's Law|Companion chatbot companies get child-safety duties, including a crisis plan and a ban on encouraging self-harm. A child harmed by a violation can sue the company. Most duties start July 1, 2027.|2027-01-01
ca SB 867|California AI Toy Pause|Toys with a built-in companion chatbot may not be made or sold in California until January 1, 2031.
wa HB 2225|Washington AI Companion Chatbot Law|Chatbot companies must tell users the chatbot is a machine, add protections for minors and keep a self-harm plan. A user hurt by a violation can sue under the state consumer protection law.|2027-01-01
ri HB 7350|Rhode Island AI Companion Law|AI companion tools must say what they are. They must also keep a plan for users who talk about suicide or about hurting someone else.|2027-01-01
id S 1297|Idaho Conversational AI Safety Act|Chatbot companies must tell users they are talking to AI and add protections for minors. Summaries of the law say the state attorney general enforces it.|2027-07-01
pa HB 507|Pennsylvania Baby Food Protection Act|The state would test each batch of baby food made in Pennsylvania for arsenic, lead, cadmium and mercury. Limits would apply, and the results would go on the label.
va HB 1844|Virginia Baby Food Protection Act|Baby food with more toxic heavy metals than the FDA allows may not be sold in Virginia. Makers must test each product monthly and post the results online.|2026-01-01
ny A 1797|New York Hair Relaxer Warning Label Law|A hair relaxer sold in New York must carry a warning label if it contains a chemical linked to cancer or reproductive harm. The label rule starts about May 2027.
ca SB 236|California C.U.R.L. Act|Hair relaxers that contain certain listed chemicals, formaldehyde among them, may not be made or sold in California. A state agency registers makers and enforces the ban.
ga SB 144|Georgia Pesticide Label Shield|This law protects pesticide makers. A Georgia lawsuit cannot claim a maker failed to warn of health risks if the label met federal rules. News reports say it does not reach cases already filed.
ca AB 56|California Social Media Warning Label Law|Social media platforms must show users under 18 a mental health warning. It appears at first use each day, again after three hours, and every hour after that.|2027-01-01
pa HB 2883|Pennsylvania PFAS Pesticide Ban|This bill would ban the sale and use of pesticides that contain PFAS when safer choices exist. PFAS from farm fields can reach drinking water.
